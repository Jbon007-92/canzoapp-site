Privacy policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

Canzo (formerly FretNought) is a songwriting app for Android, iPhone, Windows and Mac, made by Bonura Studios LLC ("we"). This page explains what Canzo and this website keep about you and why. The short version: your songs and recordings stay on your device, Canzo has no account, no ads and no tracking, and we only hear from you when you write to us.

Your songs and recordings

Songs, recordings and settings are saved on your own device, in Canzo's storage. They are not uploaded to us or to anyone else. Files you export (a chord chart, a backup, a recording) go wherever you choose to save or send them.

The microphone

Canzo asks to use the microphone only when you record a take or ask it to hear a chord. The sound is processed on your device and kept there, as part of your song. It is never sent to us.

Sharing a song

When you share a song, the whole song travels inside the link you send, including the name you sign it with and any comments on it. The part of the link that holds the song is never sent to any server, ours included. Opening a song link on canzoapp.com reads it in your browser only.

Reporting a problem

Report a problem in the app writes an email for you to send from your own email app. It holds what you typed, Canzo's version, your device's model and system, and the song only if you choose to include it. Nothing is sent unless you send that email. We use it only to fix the problem, and delete it when it is no longer needed.

Joining the beta and writing to us

If you join the beta or write to us, we keep your email address and what you wrote, to send you the beta, answer you and tell you about Canzo. We never sell it or share it for advertising. Ask us to remove it at any time and we will. When we use a mailing service to send beta news, it holds your address on our behalf and every email has a link to leave the list.

The app stores

Canzo is installed through Google Play and Apple's App Store (TestFlight during the beta). Google and Apple collect information under their own privacy policies, and may share crash reports and usage statistics with us when you allow it on your device. These never include your songs.

This website

canzoapp.com sets no cookies and uses no analytics, ads or trackers, and loads nothing from other websites. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, sees your device's internet address when it sends you a page and keeps it for a short time for security.

Children

Canzo is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes and contact

When this policy changes we update this page and its date, and say so in the app before anything new is collected. Questions or requests: info@bonurastudios.com, Bonura Studios LLC.
