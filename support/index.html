Support

We read every message. Write to support@bonurastudios.com.

Report a problem from the app

In Canzo, open Settings (or the ☰ menu) and tap Report a problem. Say what you did and what went wrong; your email app then opens with the report written, including which version of Canzo you have and which device it runs on. Nothing is sent until you press send there.

Opening a song someone sent you

A shared song travels inside its link. Tap the link to see the song's sections and chords. To open it in Canzo, copy the link, open Chart and share in Canzo and paste it into Open link.

Moving from FretNought

Canzo was called FretNought. Your songs come with you: in the old app, open My songs and choose Back up all songs, then in Canzo choose Add songs from a backup. Old song links still open.

Keeping your songs safe

Your songs live on your device. Back them up now and then from My songs, and keep the file somewhere safe.
